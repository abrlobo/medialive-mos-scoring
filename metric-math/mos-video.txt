MOS-Video — CloudWatch Metric Math
==================================

Add these metrics to a CloudWatch graph for your MediaLive channel.
Period: 60 seconds. Statistic as shown. Set each metric's Id as listed.

  Id   Metric                          Statistic   Requires
  ---  ------------------------------  ----------  --------------------
  m8   DroppedFrames                   Sum         —
  m9   FillMsec                        Maximum     —
  m10  SvqTime                         Maximum     —
  m11  MinMQCS                         Minimum     CMAF Ingest output
  m12  MqcsVideoFrameDrops             Minimum     CMAF Ingest output
  m13  MqcsFreezeFrameDetected         Minimum     CMAF Ingest output
  m14  MqcsSvq                         Minimum     CMAF Ingest output
  m15  MqcsBlackFrameDetected          Minimum     CMAF Ingest output
  m16  MqcsFillFrameInsertion          Minimum     CMAF Ingest output
  m17  MqcsContinuityCounterErrors     Minimum     CMAF Ingest output

Dimensions: ChannelId, Pipeline.

The Mqcs* metrics require a CMAF Ingest output group on the channel. The
ISPRESENT guards below make the expression safe without one.


Expressions
-----------
Display only MOS_V; keep the components hidden until a drop needs
decomposing.

  drop_rate    IF(m8 > 0, m8/(30*60), 0)
  vfd_norm     IF(ISPRESENT(m12), (100-m12)/100, 0)
  FDS          1 - MIN(1, drop_rate*0.5 + vfd_norm*0.5)

  freeze_norm  IF(ISPRESENT(m13), m13/100, 1)
  fill_pen     MIN(1, m9/30000)
  FFS          freeze_norm*0.7 + (1-fill_pen)*0.3

  svq_norm     IF(ISPRESENT(m14), m14/100, 1)
  EQS          (1 - MIN(1, m10/100))*0.5 + svq_norm*0.5

  black_norm   IF(ISPRESENT(m15), m15/100, 1)
  fillfr_norm  IF(ISPRESENT(m16), m16/100, 1)
  cc_norm      IF(ISPRESENT(m17), m17/100, 1)
  CIS          black_norm*0.35 + fillfr_norm*0.35 + cc_norm*0.30

  MNS          IF(ISPRESENT(m11), m11/100, 1)

  VQI          FDS*0.35 + FFS*0.25 + EQS*0.20 + CIS*0.15 + MNS*0.05
  MOS_V        MAX(1.0, MIN(4.5, 1.0 + POW(VQI, 0.9) * 3.5))


ADJUST BEFORE USE
-----------------
The drop_rate expression assumes 30 fps over a 60-second period:

    IF(m8 > 0, m8/(30*60), 0)
                  ^^
Change this to your channel's output frame rate. Common values:

    25 fps   ->  m8/(25*60)
    30 fps   ->  m8/(30*60)      (as written)
    50 fps   ->  m8/(50*60)
    60 fps   ->  m8/(60*60)

Leaving it at 30 on a 60 fps channel understates the drop rate by half.


Component weights
-----------------
  FDS  35%   frame continuity      most noticeable to viewers
  FFS  25%   freeze events
  EQS  20%   encoder quality stress
  CIS  15%   content integrity
  MNS   5%   MinMQCS cross-check   components already counted individually

These sum to 1.0. They derive from operational experience rather than a
scored subjective dataset — calibrate against your own QC observations
before trusting a threshold in production.
